Questions, answered

Why does Android say "File might be harmful"?

Android browsers show this for any APK downloaded outside Google Play. Velvet Music has no ads and no trackers. Tap Download anyway to continue.

What can I do in Velvet Peoples and messaging?

Create an optional profile, post thoughts with a song attached, follow other listeners, get notified about likes and replies, and message friends. Offline playback stays private.

Which audio formats and effects are supported?

FLAC, MP3, WAV, AAC, M4A, OGG and OPUS. Effects include 8D and 16D spatial audio, Slowed + Reverb, an ambient sound mixer, Smart Auto-Mix DJ mode and A-B looping.

Is Velvet Music free? Are there ads?

It's completely free, with no ads, popups or banners.
