# Velvet Music — Precision Audio Engineering

Velvet Music is an ultra-premium, studio-grade offline audio workstation architected for Android. Engineered without cloud dependencies, telemetry tracking, or third-party advertisements, every digital signal processing (DSP) stage executes natively on local device hardware with 32-bit floating-point precision.

---

## Architectural Highlights

- **32-Bit Floating-Point DSP Engine**: 10-Band precision linear-phase filtering with dynamic headroom management, preventing digital clipping across sub-bass and high-frequency excursions.
- **8D Orbital Spatial Acoustics**: Spherical soundstage simulation utilizing binaural panning and studio-calibrated room impulse convolution.
- **Abbey Road Reverb Filtering**: Strategic low-frequency high-pass cutoff on reverberant paths, ensuring low-end kick punch remains clean and unclipped in the dry channel.
- **Master-Grade Offline Export**: Direct hardware rendering of customized acoustic curves, tempo adjustments, and room responses into lossless standard audio formats.
- **Sovereign Privacy Architecture**: Strict zero-telemetry policy. Your local audio collection remains completely sovereign to your device storage.
